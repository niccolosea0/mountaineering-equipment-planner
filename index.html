    Safety before shopping

    Good equipment supports good systems.

    This planner organizes purchases; it does not teach anchor building, cleaning, belaying, rappelling, or rescue skills. Learn those with a qualified instructor or experienced mentor.

    Explore UIAA safety resources ↗
    01

    Know the history

    Avoid used ropes, harnesses, helmets, slings, and PAS systems when you cannot verify their full history.

    02

    Check compatibility

    Confirm rope diameter ranges, carabiner shapes, and every manufacturer instruction before combining equipment.

    03

    Inspect every trip

    Look for sheath damage, deformation, sharp edges, corrosion, faded webbing, damaged stitching, and recall notices.

    04

    Buy certified gear

    Use reputable retailers and look for relevant UIAA and/or CE markings on life-safety equipment.

    05

    Keep steel in its lane

    Steel lockers suit high-wear anchor points. Use a compatible dedicated HMS locker with your Reverso for belaying.

    06

    Train before relying

    Practice sport-anchor cleaning, rappelling, guide mode, and partner checks under qualified supervision.
